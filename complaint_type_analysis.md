# NYC 311 Complaint Type Analysis

## Most Common Complaint Type

After filtering the NYC 311 dataset to incidents created in 2024 with a ZIP code, Illegal Parking was the most common complaint type, with 505,546 records.

## Comparison Between Periods

Illegal Parking complaints were counted across all boroughs for two periods:

| Period | Illegal Parking complaints |
|---|---:|
| January–February 2024 | 79,916 |
| June–July 2024 | 86,237 |

June–July had 6,321 more complaints than January–February, an increase of approximately 7.9%.

## Interpretation

Illegal Parking complaints were more frequent in June–July than in January–February in this dataset. This comparison describes the observed complaint counts; it does not by itself explain why the difference occurred.
